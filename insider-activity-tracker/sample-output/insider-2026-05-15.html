How to read this page

  • Form 4 is the SEC filing that corporate insiders — officers, directors, and 10% owners — must submit within two business days of transacting in their company's stock.
  • Open-market buying on this page refers strictly to open-market purchases reported on Form 4 (transaction code P). It excludes stock-comp grants, option exercises, and tax-related dispositions.
  • Open-market selling on this page refers strictly to open-market sales reported on Form 4 (transaction code S). It excludes grants, gifts, and option-related dispositions.
  • Total dollar value is computed as shares times reported price across the filings in the week.
  • Sector is the company's classification, not the insider's.
  • Distinct filers is the count of unique people who filed in the week for that company on that side.

None of these are direction calls. They are descriptions of past transactions.
