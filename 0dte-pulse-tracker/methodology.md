# 0DTE Pulse — Methodology

## What this page is

The 0DTE Pulse is an end-of-day journal page summarising today's
zero-day-to-expiration options tape. It is published once per weekday,
after the close, at 5 PM ET. It is not a feed and it is not a watchlist
of names to trade.

The page records what the tape looked like today — which tickers
attracted the heaviest 0DTE notable prints, how the dollar weight
split between calls and puts, and how today's 0DTE activity sat
relative to the broader options tape. It does not project, advise, or
predict.

## What "0DTE" means

A 0DTE option is one whose final trading day is today. It expires at
today's close. Once the day is over, the contract is either exercised,
assigned, or worthless. Because the entire P&L of a 0DTE position
resolves inside one trading session, the contracts are exceptionally
path-dependent and the vast majority expire worthless. The 0DTE tape
is one of the noisiest surfaces in the market.

The page treats only contracts whose listed expiry equals today's date
as 0DTE. Anything dated for a later expiry is filtered out before it
enters the pulse.

## What "notable prints" means on this page

The page does not show every 0DTE trade — that would be unreadable.
It shows the slice of activity that surfaced today as unusual on size,
premium, or fill profile. The exact thresholds are internal and not
published.

The point of the filter is not to define what a "good" trade looks
like. It is to keep the page focused on the trades that stood out
enough from typical activity to be worth recording in a journal entry.

## What the page reports

For each ticker that cleared the bar today, the page reports:

- **0DTE notable prints today** — the count of qualifying trades on that
  ticker's 0DTE chain.
- **Total premium** — the sum of dollar premium across those prints.
- **Call share / put share** — the dollar weight of call-side versus
  put-side prints. Both shares are reported so the reader sees the
  split, not a single ratio.
- **Tilt** — a one-word descriptor of where the dollar weight landed:
  heavily call-tilted, call-tilted, balanced, put-tilted, or heavily
  put-tilted. The thresholds for those labels are internal and not
  published.

The page also reports two market-wide context numbers:

- **Share of today's total options dollar volume** — qualifying 0DTE
  notable premium divided by today's total options dollar volume
  across the market.
- **Market-wide call/put dollar tilt today** — how the broader options
  tape split between calls and puts today.

## What the bands and tilts mean

The tilts are descriptive labels for today's tape. They are not
ratings, and they are not direction calls. A ticker showing
"heavily call-tilted" does not mean the page is suggesting the price
will rise. It means today's qualifying 0DTE dollar weight on that
ticker was concentrated on the call side. That can resolve in either
direction or fade into nothing tomorrow.

## What this page deliberately does not do

- It does not recommend or advise action on any name listed.
- It does not project the size, direction, or timing of any future
  price move.
- It does not stream tape data. The page is a single end-of-day
  snapshot, refreshed once per weekday at 5 PM ET.
- It does not disclose the exact thresholds used to qualify prints,
  nor the data sources behind the readings.
- It does not show every 0DTE trade — only the qualifying notable
  prints rolled up per ticker.

If you find yourself reading this page as a list of names to chase
intraday tomorrow, please re-read this section. 0DTE options are
exceptionally high-risk; most expire worthless. The page is a
description of conditions, not a list of trades.

## Cadence

Published once each weekday at 5 PM ET. The "as of" stamp on the
page is the snapshot time, not the time you opened the browser. If a
feed upstream of the page degrades, the page shows a data-quality
note and the previous evening's snapshot remains in place.
