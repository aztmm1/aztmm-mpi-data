How to read this page

  • 0DTE means options whose final trading day is today — they expire at today's close.
  • Notable prints are options trades that surfaced as unusual by size, premium, or fill profile relative to typical activity on that contract. The threshold is internal and not disclosed here.
  • Call share / put share is the dollar weight of call-side versus put-side notable prints for that ticker today.
  • Tilt is a one-word descriptor of where the dollar weight of today's 0DTE prints landed for each ticker.
  • Share of today's total options dollar volume is the dollar weight of qualifying 0DTE notable prints divided by today's total options dollar volume across the market.

None of these are direction calls. They are descriptions of conditions on today's tape.
