How to read this page

  • Upcoming reporter means a company whose earnings release is on the calendar for one of the next five trading days. The "today" date itself is excluded because those reports have already happened by the time this page publishes.
  • Notable options interest refers to a per-alert dollar and contract floor on today's options tape. The threshold itself is internal and not disclosed here.
  • Call share / put share is the dollar weight of call-side versus put-side qualifying alerts for that ticker today.
  • Tilt is a one-word descriptor of where the dollar weight of today's qualifying alerts landed.
  • Reporting column shows the day-of-week label and the calendar date. "Before the open" and "after the close" describe whether the company reports premarket or postmarket.

None of these are direction calls. They are descriptions of conditions on today's tape paired with calendar facts.
