How to read this page

  • Short interest as a share of float is the number of shares reported short divided by shares available to trade.
  • Days to cover is the reported short interest divided by average daily volume — a rough measure of how compressed the short position is.
  • Call/put options interest on this page is the ratio of call-side to put-side options interest seen tonight. A high ratio means call-side interest dominated.
  • Bands (Elevated / Notable / Watching) summarise where each name sits in tonight's screen relative to the others.

None of these are direction calls. They are descriptions of conditions on tonight's tape.
