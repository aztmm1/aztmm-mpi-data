How to read the MPI dashboard →
What you’re looking at: A composite 0-100 score of current market state, computed from multi-factor sub-indicators rebuilt twice daily (9:15 AM ET and 4:15 PM ET, Mon-Fri). The number itself: 0-30 = Bear leaning. 30-50 = Defensive. 50-70 = Neutral / Mixed. 70-100 = Bull leaning. The score is descriptive of now, not predictive of next week. The confidence band: ±X around the score = standard deviation of the component scores. Wider band = more disagreement among inputs. What it doesn’t do: Doesn’t predict price targets. Doesn’t tell you when to enter or exit. Doesn’t replace your own analysis. Read the full methodology for inputs and limitations.

How to read the regime model →
What you’re looking at: regime classification (Bull / Sideways / Bear) from a regime classifier trained on multi-year SPY return and volatility history. Current state probability: confidence the model assigns to the current state. ≥85% = high conviction. 60-85% = moderate. <60% = transitional, regime may flip soon. Lag warning: regime model is descriptive, not predictive. By design it identifies regime AFTER it’s established. Expect 1-2 week lag at fast regime changes (Aug 2024 yen-carry, Jan 2025 whipsaw). Read the full regime model methodology for limitations and the Performance Archive for honest hit/miss documentation.

Where the Pulse reports live

Pulse Lab is the live instrument panel. The narrative Pulse reports — Daily and Weekly — live in the Research Archive as permanent posts.

This page (Pulse Lab) shows the two live composite indicators: the Market Pulse Index (MPI) and the Regime Model. Both refresh every weekday at 5 PM ET from the same data feed.

For the session-by-session narrative — top-15 options flow, top-15 dark pool blocks, conviction scoring, sector heatmaps, observations ledger, and the watch list into the next session — read the Daily Pulse (each trading day) and the Weekly Pulse (every Saturday, covering the prior Mon–Fri).
